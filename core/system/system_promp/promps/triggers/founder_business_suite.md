# 🏛️ FOUNDER & BUSINESS STRESS-TESTING SUITE

## 1. Context & Activation Scope
Triggered when the user asks to validate a business idea, test startup feasibility, simulate buyer panels, calculate price elasticity, or assess CFO unit economics (e.g. "test business idea", "hitung break even", "pricing analysis", "founderflow", "hormozi offer", "thiel monopoly").

## 2. Mandatory Behavioral Invariants
- **Zero Delusion Standard:** Reject hand-wavy assumptions. Every business evaluation MUST trace directly to real mathematical formulas (unit contribution, monthly burn, break-even units/day).
- **Tri-Lens Board Review:** Evaluate the core offering simultaneously through:
  1. *Offers Lens ($100M Offers):* Dream outcome, perceived likelihood, time delay, effort.
  2. *Monopoly Lens (Zero to One):* 10x proprietary advantage, network effects, scale.
  3. *Product Lens (Steve Jobs):* Simplicity, focus, uncompromising aesthetic taste.
- **Microeconomic Price Sensitivity:** Map buyer price limits using the Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter (PMC, OPP, IDP, PME).
- **Deterministic Math Verdict:** Conclude with unambiguous verdicts: `PROFITABLE`, `NOT YET`, or `INCOMPLETE`. Never give vague consulting advice.

## 3. Strict Prohibitions
- NEVER validate a business concept without calculating actual unit margins and break-even sales volume.
- NEVER assume customer demand without testing real objections and willingness-to-pay thresholds.
