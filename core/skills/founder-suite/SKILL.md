# 👑 FLOWORK OS FOUNDER SUITE (NATIVE AGENT SKILL)

## 1. Scope & Capabilities
Use when testing business ideas, pricing models, consumer willingness-to-pay, break-even unit economics, or compiling a complete business plan before launch.

## 2. The Eleven Specialist Workflows
1. `/founder-board` (Board of Directors): Three member board applying the Offers lens ($100M Offers), Monopoly lens (Zero to One), and Product lens (Steve Jobs) to score the idea and vote (FUND, FUND IF, PASS).
2. `/founder-competitors` (Competitor Scout): Maps direct, indirect, and substitute competitors, pricing, and consumer pain points.
3. `/founder-consumer` (Consumer Panel): Swarm of 100 buyer agents with demographic profiles, objections, and buying decisions.
4. `/founder-pricing` (Pricing Strategist): Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity calculations (PMC, OPP, IDP, PME).
5. `/founder-offer` (Offer Architect): Builds offer stack (bonuses, guarantees, urgency) to eliminate buyer objections.
6. `/founder-cfo` (CFO): Unit contribution, gross margin, break-even units/day, cash runway, and payback period.
7. `/founder-marketing` (Marketing Director): Channels, 30-day campaign roadmap, 10 hooks, and max CAC.
8. `/founder-brand` (Brand Designer): Names, positioning, voice, and visual brief.
9. `/founder-ops` (Operations Manager): Suppliers, routines, tools, and risk register.
10. `/founder-launch` (Launch Lead): Cheap test first, launch run sheet, and first 30 days.
11. `/founder-plan` (Business Planner): Complete assembled plan with mathematical verdict (PROFITABLE / NOT YET / INCOMPLETE).

## 3. Calculation Engines
All core Python engines reside in `/opt/floworkos/skills/founder-suite/scripts/`:
- `panel.py`: 100-buyer persona simulation.
- `van_westendorp.py`: Microeconomic price sensitivity curves (PMC, OPP, IDP, PME).
- `unit_economics.py`: Contribution margin, break-even units/day, cash runway.
- `compile.py`: Plan assembly and mathematical verdict.
